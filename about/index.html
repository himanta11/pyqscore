About PYQScore

Empowering Aspirants Through Smarter Online Exam Preparation

PYQScore is an online examination preparation and practice platform and the digital learning initiative of Scholar Academy of Learning, an educational institute based in Dibrugarh, Assam.

We aim to make competitive examination preparation more accessible, convenient and effective for students through online mock tests, previous-year questions (PYQs), multiple-choice questions (MCQs) and digital learning resources.

Designed with the needs of competitive exam aspirants in mind, PYQScore helps students practise questions, strengthen their subject knowledge and prepare with greater confidence.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to make quality online exam practice accessible to every aspiring student, regardless of their location.

We believe that consistent practice, proper revision and regular self-assessment are essential parts of competitive examination preparation. Through PYQScore, we aim to create a learning environment where students can practise, evaluate their preparation and continuously improve.

🌟

Our Vision

We envision PYQScore becoming a trusted online examination practice platform for students in Assam and across India.

We want to help aspirants move beyond passive learning and develop a consistent habit of practising questions, reviewing performance and improving their preparation.

Practise More. Prepare Better. Achieve Your Goals.

Our Connection with Scholar Academy of Learning

PYQScore operates as the online learning platform of Scholar Academy of Learning, an educational institute with a presence in Dibrugarh and Tezpur, Assam.

Building on our educational experience, we developed PYQScore to extend learning opportunities beyond the traditional classroom. While Scholar Academy of Learning provides coaching and classroom education, PYQScore focuses on online question practice and mock-test preparation.

Our goal is to serve not only our classroom students but also competitive examination aspirants from across Assam and other parts of India.
