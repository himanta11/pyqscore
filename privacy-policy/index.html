Overview & Core Philosophy

PYQScore (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an exam preparation and practice platform designed to assist competitive exam aspirants—specifically for the Assam Direct Recruitment Examination (ADRE 3.0), APSC, and related competitive examinations—through active recall, high-yield Past Year Questions (PYQs), timed state-rank mock tests, and bilingual explanations (Assamese & English).

Our On-Device First, Privacy-Respecting Architecture:

🛡️

Zero Data Monetization

We never sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal data, notes, or study statistics to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

⚡

Minimal Cloud Footprint

Core practice questions, quiz sessions, accuracy metrics, and question banks function locally on your device without requiring continuous cloud telemetry.

🔍

Transparent Permissions

Every Android permission requested has a direct, indispensable educational purpose. We do not inspect, intercept, or monitor any other applications on your device.

3

Information We Collect and How We Use It

A. Guest Mode (No Account Required)

You can use PYQScore without creating an account. In Guest Mode:

  • Zero Personal Identifiable Information (PII) is collected or transmitted.
  • All question progress, accuracy rates, study streaks, and test scores are saved strictly on your device inside an offline SQLite database.

B. Account Creation & Cloud Sync (Optional)

If you optionally sign in (via Google Sign-In or Email through Supabase):

  • Information Collected: Display name, email address, and unique user identifier (UID).
  • Purpose: To authenticate your identity, sync your study streaks, test results, bookmarks, and verify your Pro Pass subscription across your devices.

C. Financial & Payment Information

  • All payments (Monthly Pass ₹129, 3-Month Pro Pass ₹249) are processed exclusively by Google Play In-App Billing.
  • PYQScore does not receive, process, or store your credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or bank account credentials.
  • Google Play securely validates your transaction and supplies our app with a cryptographic purchase token to verify your active plan.

Data Retention & User Rights

You maintain complete ownership and control over your study data:

Instant Local Data Wipe:

You can erase all local progress, bookmarks, and quiz statistics at any time inside the app via Profile → Preferences → Reset All App Data.

Account Deletion Right:

If you have created an online account and wish to permanently delete your profile, credentials, and synced records from Supabase, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing pyqscoreapp@gmail.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”. Your account data will be permanently purged within 48 business hours.

Children’s Privacy

PYQScore is built as an educational exam preparation tool for students and aspirants. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor under 13 has provided personal details without parental consent, please contact us immediately at pyqscoreapp@gmail.com, and we will delete the data.
