Proven Preparation Strategy

Why Should You Practise ADRE Previous Year Question Papers?

Solving previous year question papers is an important part of competitive examination preparation. Here are the core benefits of practising ADRE Grade 3 PYQs:

1

Understand the Question Pattern

Previous year papers help you become familiar with different question formats and the way questions are presented in the examination.

2

Identify Important Topics

By practising questions from different years, you can identify topics that deserve additional attention during your preparation.

3

Improve Time Management

Solving a complete question paper within a fixed time helps you develop better time-management skills under real exam pressure.

4

Evaluate Your Preparation

Compare your answers with the available answer keys to identify your strengths and areas that need improvement.

5

Build Confidence

Regular practice helps you become more comfortable with examination-style questions and improves your confidence on exam day.

Syllabus & Structure

ADRE Grade 3 Exam Pattern and Subjects

Before attempting previous year question papers, candidates should understand the examination scheme applicable to their particular post and recruitment notification.

The examination pattern, number of questions, total marks, duration and negative-marking rules may vary depending on the post and official notification.

Important Subjects for ADRE Grade 3 Preparation:

1 General Knowledge and Current Affairs

Indian History and Geography Assam History and Geography Indian Polity and Constitution Assam Art, Culture and Heritage National Parks and Wildlife Sanctuaries of Assam Important national and international events Government schemes and initiatives

2 General English

Vocabulary Synonyms and Antonyms Idioms and Phrases Prepositions Spotting Errors Active and Passive Voice Sentence Correction Reading Comprehension

3 Elementary Mathematics and Quantitative Aptitude

Percentage Ratio and Proportion Profit and Loss Simple and Compound Interest Time and Work Time, Speed and Distance Average Number System Basic Mensuration

4 Logical Reasoning and Mental Ability

Number Series Alphabet Series Coding and Decoding Blood Relations Direction Sense Analogy Classification Venn Diagrams

5 Social Studies and Reading Comprehension

Indian Constitution Indian Society Social and Cultural Heritage Basic Social Science Reading Passages Comprehension and Interpretation
Note: This is a general preparation topic list, not a substitute for the official syllabus for a particular ADRE Grade 3 post. Always verify specific marks and post-wise criteria from the State Level Recruitment Commission (SLRC) notification.
