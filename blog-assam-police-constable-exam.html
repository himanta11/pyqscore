Securing the uniform of an Assam Police Constable—whether in the Unarmed Branch (UB) or the Armed Branch (AB)—is one of the most respected and sought-after career goals for young aspirants across Assam. Conducted under the authority of the State Level Police Recruitment Board (SLPRB), Assam, this recruitment process selects candidates based on a balanced assessment of physical endurance and academic merit.

With hundreds of thousands of applicants competing for limited vacancies across battalions and police stations, relying on last-minute preparation is no longer viable. A high cut-off requires a structured, step-by-step strategy covering ground fitness, conceptual subject mastery, and regular timed mock testing.

1. Selection Process: The 4 Critical Stages

The SLPRB recruitment drive follows an objective, transparent, multi-stage elimination process:

01

Document Verification & Scrutiny

Immediate verification of identity, age proofs, educational qualifications (HSLC / 10+2), and a valid Assam Employment Exchange registration certificate.

02

Physical Standard Test (PST)

Measurement of height and chest expansion. This phase is purely qualifying in nature — no graded marks are awarded.

03

Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

Running and long jump assessments where graded marks are awarded that directly impact the final merit rank.

04

Written Examination (OMR-Based)

A 100-question multiple-choice test evaluating arithmetic, reasoning, language, and general awareness.

05

Viva-Voce & Final Document Scrutiny

Verification of special quota certificates (NCC, Sports, Home Guards) before the publication of the final merit list.

2. Physical Standards (PST) and Efficiency Tests (PET)

Fitness preparation must start months before the written exam notification. The PST is purely qualifying; the PET awards graded marks that directly affect your final rank.

📏 PST Standards – Height & Chest Requirements

Female candidates are completely exempt from chest measurements.

5. Step-by-Step 90-Day Preparation Timetable

A structured 90-day plan divided into three distinct phases will ensure you cover physical fitness, theory mastery, and full-length exam simulation.

01

Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1–30)

Run 3–4 km alternate mornings, practice long-jump, build core theory (Assam History, Geography, English grammar), solve 20–30 basic math problems daily.

02

Phase 2: Sectional Mastery (Days 31–60)

Focus on Reasoning puzzles, Speed-Math, Indian Polity. Solve chapter-wise PYQs. Take two timed sectional tests per week.

03

Phase 3: Full-Length Exam Simulation (Days 61–90)

Attempt full-length OMR mocks every alternate day. Taper physical intensity. Rapid revision of factual notes and formula sheets.

8. Practical OMR Exam-Day Guidelines

Your OMR sheet handling can make or break your score. Follow these golden rules on exam day:

  1. 1 Shade in blocks: Never wait until the final 10 minutes to shade all 100 answers. Bubble in blocks of 15–20 questions at a time.
  2. 2 Use proper pens: Use broad-tip ballpoint pens (0.7mm–1.0mm) for clear, machine-readable bubbles.
  3. 3 Check row alignment: Double-check row alignment between your test booklet and the OMR sheet after every block of questions.
  4. 4 Attempt every question: With no negative marking, leaving any question blank is leaving free marks on the table.
📌
Remember: There is no negative marking in the Assam Police Constable written exam. Attempting every single question is a non-negotiable strategy.
