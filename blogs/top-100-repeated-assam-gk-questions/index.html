Assam state competitive examinations—including ADRE (Grade III & IV), APSC CCE, Assam Police (SI & Constable), and Forest Guard recruitment—place heavy weightage on state-specific General Knowledge. Out of total questions in general awareness sections, nearly 30% to 45% focus directly on Assam’s history, geography, wildlife, polity, literature, and art.

A close analysis of past question trends reveals that the State Level Recruitment Commission (SLRC) and APSC frequently repeat questions based on standard foundational events and state facts. Instead of skimming through hundreds of pages of unorganized reference notes right before test day, mastering these repeatedly asked questions gives you guaranteed marks and an immediate scoring edge.

Module 1

1. High-Yield Assam History & Ancient Heritage

Q1

Who was the founder of the Ahom Kingdom in Assam?

Answer: Chaolung Sukaphaa (established in 1228 AD after entering Assam via the Patkai Hills).
Q2

In which year was the historic Battle of Saraighat fought?

Answer: 1671 AD (between the Ahom Kingdom led by General Lachit Borphukan and the Mughal Empire led by Raja Ram Singh I).
Q3

Who was the only Assamese representative to take part in the Round Table Conference in London?

Answer: Chandradhar Barua.
Q4

Who was the first Ahom king to formally adopt a Hindu title?

Answer: Jayadhwaj Singha (adopted the Hindu name Jayadhwaj Singha), while Suhungmung popularised the title Swarganarayan.
Q5

The Treaty of Yandaboo was signed in which year, ending Burmese rule in Assam?

Answer: February 24, 1826 (marking the formal advent of the British East India Company in Assam).
Q6

Who was the leader of the historic 1861 Phulaguri Dhawa peasant uprising?

Answer: Led collectively by local Tiwa and Lalung community peasants in Nagaon against the British poppy cultivation ban and oppressive taxation.
Q7

Who was crowned as the first king of the Koch dynasty?

Answer: Viswa Singha (father of Maharaja Naranarayana and General Chilarai).
Q8

In which year did the Patharughat peasant massacre take place?

Answer: January 28, 1894 in Darrang district (frequently described as the Jallianwala Bagh of Assam).
Q9

Who composed the famous historical chronicles known as Buranjis?

Answer: Official state scribes and chroniclers under royal patronage in the Ahom kingdom, initially in the Ahom language and later in Assamese prose.
Q10

Who was the first Chief Minister (Premier) of post-independence Assam?

Answer: Lokopriya Gopinath Bordoloi (awarded Bharat Ratna posthumously in 1999).
Module 2

2. Geography, River Systems & Environment

Q11

What is the total geographic area of Assam?

Answer: 78,438 square kilometers (accounting for ~2.39% of India's total land area).
Q12

Which national park in Assam is universally recognised for the highest density of the Greater One-Horned Rhinoceros?

Answer: Kaziranga National Park (declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1985 and a Tiger Reserve in 2006).
Q13

How many National Parks are currently recognized in Assam?

Answer: 7 National Parks: Kaziranga, Manas, Nameri, Orang, Dibru-Saikhowa, Raimona (6th), and Dihing Patkai (7th).
Q14

Which is the largest river island in the world located on the Brahmaputra River?

Answer: Majuli (also declared the first island district of India in 2016).
Q15

Which is the smallest inhabited river island in the world, situated near Guwahati?

Answer: Umananda Island (also called Peacock Island by the British), home to the Umananda Temple.
Q16

Where does the Brahmaputra River enter Assam after originating in Tibet?

Answer: Near Sadiya, following the confluence of the Siang (Dihang), Dibang, and Lohit rivers.
Q17

Which district of Assam is renowned for the highest tea production acreage?

Answer: Dibrugarh District (widely hailed as the Tea City of India).
Q18

Where was Asia's first oil refinery set up in 1901?

Answer: Digboi, Tinsukia district (often called the birthplace of the Indian oil industry).
Q19

The famous hot spring known as Garampani is located in which wildlife sanctuary of Assam?

Answer: Garampani Wildlife Sanctuary in Karbi Anglong district.
Q20

Which hill range forms the natural boundary separating Assam from Nagaland?

Answer: Barail Range.
Module 3

3. Assam Art, Culture, Literature & Socio-Religious Movements

Q21

Who is the father of the Neo-Vaishnavite movement and creator of Sattriya dance in Assam?

Answer: Mahapurush Srimanta Sankardeva (1449–1568 AD).
Q22

In which year was Sattriya recognized as an official Classical Dance form by Sangeet Natak Akademi?

Answer: 15th November 2000.
Q23

Who wrote the state anthem of Assam, "O Mur Apunar Desh"?

Answer: Sahityarathi Lakshminath Bezbaroa (officially adopted as state anthem at the Assam Chhatra Sanmilan in Tezpur, 1927).
Q24

Who was the first recipient of the prestigious Jnanpith Award from Assam?

Answer: Dr. Birendra Kumar Bhattacharya (in 1979 for his acclaimed novel Mrityunjay).
Q25

Who was the second Assamese author to win the Jnanpith Award?

Answer: Mamoni Raisom Goswami (Dr. Indira Goswami) in the year 2000.
Q26

Which traditional harvest festival is celebrated by the Mising community in Assam during spring?

Answer: Ali-Ai-Ligang (celebrated on the first Wednesday of the month of Ginmur Bolo / Phalguna).
Q27

What is the first Assamese language feature film directed by Jyoti Prasad Agarwala in 1935?

Answer: Joymoti (released on 10 March 1935 at Raonak Theatre, Kolkata).
Q28

Where was the first session of the Assam Sahitya Sabha held in 1917, and who presided over it?

Answer: Held at Sivasagar; presided over by Padmanath Gohain Baruah.
Q29

Which historic terracotta architectural town in Assam is famed for terracotta craftsmanship?

Answer: Asharikandi village (Dhubri district).
Q30

Which temple atop Nilachal Hill in Guwahati serves as the epicentre for the annual Ambubachi Mela?

Answer: Maa Kamakhya Temple (reconstructed by King Naranarayana and Chilarai in 1565 AD).
Module 4

4. State Economy, Polity & Administrative Governance

Q31

How many administrative districts are there in the state of Assam?

Answer: 35 administrative districts.
Q32

How many Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha seats are allocated to Assam?

Answer: 14 Lok Sabha seats and 7 Rajya Sabha seats.
Q33

What is the total strength of elected members in the Assam Legislative Assembly?

Answer: 126 Members of the Legislative Assembly (MLAs).
Q34

Under which schedule of the Indian Constitution are the Bodoland Territorial Council (BTC) and Karbi Anglong Autonomous Council formed?

Answer: The Sixth Schedule of the Indian Constitution (Tribal areas in Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura, and Mizoram).
Q35

Where is the headquarters of the North Eastern Coalfields located in Assam?

Answer: Margherita (Tinsukia district).
Q36

In which town is the Toklai Tea Research Institute—the oldest tea research station in the world—located?

Answer: Jorhat (established in 1911 under the Tea Research Association).
Q37

Which bridge connects the districts of Dibrugarh and Dhemaji over the Brahmaputra River?

Answer: Bogibeel Bridge (measuring 4.94 km, India's longest rail-cum-road bridge).
Q38

The Dr. Bhupen Hazarika Setu (Dhola-Sadiya Bridge) is built over which river?

Answer: Lohit River (connecting Dhola in Assam with Sadiya, stretching 9.15 km).
Q39

Which flagship public healthcare and financial assistance scheme was launched by the Government of Assam for women empowerment?

Answer: Orunodoi Scheme (Orunodoi 1.0 & 2.0).
Q40

What is the official state animal and state bird of Assam?

Answer: State Animal: One-horned Rhinoceros (Rhinoceros unicornis); State Bird: White-winged Wood Duck (Asarcornis scutulata / Deo Hah).

5. How to Retain and Score High in Assam GK

Memorizing standalone questions without regular testing leads to confusion when dealing with multiple-choice questions that feature closely matching distractors:

1

Practice Active Recall

Instead of passive re-reading, cover the answers and test your brain under timed conditions. Active retrieval forces synaptical memory strengthening.

2

Attempt Full-Length Mocks

Solve bilingual question sets on PYQscore to assess your accuracy, identify weak historical themes, and master -0.25 negative marking.

3

Understand the Context

Connect historical events chronologically. For example, study the rise of the Ahom kingdom alongside parallel regional developments in the Koch and Kachari dynasties.
