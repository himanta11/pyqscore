Important Subjects for ADRE Mock Test Preparation

A balanced preparation strategy should include regular practice across the subjects mentioned in the applicable official examination syllabus.

🦏

1. Assam General Knowledge

Prepare history of Assam, geography, rivers, culture, traditions, important personalities, festivals, heritage, and administrative institutions.

📚

2. General Knowledge

Strengthen your knowledge of important national and general topics through regular revision and objective MCQ practice.

📐

3. Mathematics & Quantitative

Practise basic mathematical concepts and quantitative aptitude questions. Focus on accuracy, calculation speed and core formulas.

🧩

4. Logical Reasoning

Practise verbal and non-verbal reasoning questions to improve logical thinking, pattern recognition, and problem-solving agility.

📖

5. English Language

Revise grammar rules, vocabulary, sentence completion, prepositions, comprehension, and other relevant English topics.

🔬

6. General Science

Study fundamental concepts from Physics, Chemistry, and Biology according to the applicable Class 9 & 10 level syllabus.

💡 Preparation Tip: Do not spend all your study time on one subject. Maintain a balanced timetable and regularly revise the topics you find difficult.
